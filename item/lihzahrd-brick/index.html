Lihzahrd Brick in Terraria

Terraria item ID 1101. Terraria 1.4.5.7.

Lihzahrd Bricks are blocks that make up the structure of the Jungle Temple. They are the toughest block in the game, being impervious to explosives and only breakable using the Picksaw or a better pickaxe or drill. They also cannot be made inactive using Actuators until Plantera has been defeated, unless they are above the surface layer. Like Jungle grass, placing 140 (Desktop, Console and Mobile versions) / 80 (Old-gen console and 3DS versions) Lihzahrd Bricks in one area creates a Jungle biome.
