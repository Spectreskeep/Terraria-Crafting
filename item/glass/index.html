Glass in Terraria

Terraria item ID 170. Terraria 1.4.5.7.

Glass is crafted from different types of sand at a Furnace. Glass can be placed as a block and is an ingredient in producing various items.

How to craft Glass

Crafted at By Hand. Ingredients:

  • Glass Platform x2

How to craft Glass

Crafted at Furnace. Ingredients:

  • Any Sand x2

How to craft Glass
