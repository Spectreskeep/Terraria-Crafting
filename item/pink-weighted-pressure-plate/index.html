Pink Weighted Pressure Plate in Terraria

Terraria item ID 3626. Terraria 1.4.5.7.

Pressure Plates are activation mechanisms that trigger wired mechanisms when stepped on. A pressure plate's color determines which characters can trigger it. Red, Brown, Gray, Orange, and Lihzahrd Pressure Plates are randomly generated on the map during world creation as the trigger for traps. They only take up a very small space on top of blocks, so they may be difficult to spot. The color of naturally spawned pressure plates is dependent on the color of the block they are generated on top of. Except for Lihzahrd Pressure Plates, they can also be purchased from the Mechanic.

How to craft Pink Weighted Pressure Plate

Crafted at Tinkerer's Workshop. Ingredients:

  • Any Pressure Plate x1
  • Any Iron Bar x2
  • Wire x1
