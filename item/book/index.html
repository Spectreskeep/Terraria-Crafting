Book in Terraria

Terraria item ID 149. Terraria 1.4.5.7.

The Book is a crafting material used only to craft Bookcases, Pianos, and Librarian Blocks. It can also be placed as decoration on Flat-surface items. Books are found placed on the Dungeon's many shelves, where they can be freed with a pickaxe or drill. They can also be renewably obtained from Dungeon or Stockade Crates, and can be bought from the Wizard for 15 Silver each.
