Stone Block in Terraria

Terraria item ID 3. Terraria 1.4.5.7.

The Stone Block is a type of block that is generated naturally in very large parts of the world. The majority of the Underground and Cavern layers is comprised of Stone Blocks. As a crafting ingredient, they are vital early in the game to craft the Furnace and Wooden Arrows. After Hardmode is activated in a world, nearby Corrupt, Crimson, and Hallowed blocks can spread through Stone Blocks, changing them into Ebonstone, Crimstone, or Pearlstone, respectively.

How to craft Stone Block

Crafted at Chlorophyte Extractinator. Ingredients:

  • Pearlstone Block x1
