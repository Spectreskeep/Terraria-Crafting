Musket Ball in Terraria

Terraria item ID 97. Terraria 1.4.5.7.

The Musket Ball is a pre-Hardmode bullet. It has no special properties and is used for crafting a variety of more advanced ammunition. When either Musket Balls or Tungsten Bullets are fired with certain guns, they will be converted into a special projectile (e.g. the Uzi converting them into High Velocity Bullets). Some other guns, such as the Pew-matic Horn, can convert any type of bullet. Musket Balls can be bought from the Arms Dealer for 7 Copper each, or 70 Silver for 1000 rounds.

What Musket Ball is used to craft

  • Chlorophyte Bullet
  • Crystal Bullet
  • Cursed Bullet
  • Endless Musket Pouch
  • Ichor Bullet
  • Meteor Shot
  • Silver Bullet
  • Tungsten Bullet
