Cog in Terraria

Terraria item ID 1344. Terraria 1.4.5.7.

Cogs are Hardmode crafting materials and placeable blocks that are purchased from the Steampunker for 7 Silver each. They are used to make High Velocity Bullets, Liquid Sensors, and Steampunk furniture. When Cogs are placed together they will display animated machinery. Corners, lines, and junctions will never be animated, though blocks inside the group of Cogs and the left border will always be animated and other borders are sometimes animated with a 2/3 (66.67%) chance. Crafting all Steampunk furniture will require 159 () / 153 ( ) / 33 () Cogs.
