Bottled Water in Terraria

Terraria item ID 126. Terraria 1.4.5.7.

Bottled Water is a pre-Hardmode crafting material and recovery potion. It can be crafted at a sink or a body of water. Bottled Water mainly serves as the basis of almost every craftable potion in the game, and is also required for crafting flasks. When used, it restores 30 health and inflicts Potion Sickness for 45 seconds. This duration can be reduced via the Philosopher's Stone or Charm of Myths.

How to craft Bottled Water

Crafted at Water / Sink. Ingredients:

  • Bottle x1
