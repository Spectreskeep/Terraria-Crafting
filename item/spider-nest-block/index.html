Spider Nest Block in Terraria

Terraria item ID 4139. Terraria 1.4.5.7.

Spider Nest Blocks are Hardmode blocks crafted from one Spider Fang and 10 Cobwebs at a Work Bench. They are used to craft Spider furniture and Spider Nest Walls. Crafting all items requires 153 Spider Nest Blocks.

How to craft Spider Nest Block

Crafted at By Hand. Ingredients:

  • Spider Platform x2

How to craft Spider Nest Block

Crafted at Work Bench, makes 10. Ingredients:

  • Cobweb x10
  • Spider Fang x1

How to craft Spider Nest Block

Crafted at Work Bench. Ingredients:

  • Spider Nest Wall x4
